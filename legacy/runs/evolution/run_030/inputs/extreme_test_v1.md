# Viral Premise Guide — Extreme Diagnostic Test v1

## Test Scope

This is an isolated diagnostic guide. It is not a production replacement.

Its purpose is to test whether the model can create bold, character-centered, scroll-stopping 2B premises when visual attraction, teasing energy and a provocative situation are explicitly prioritized.

Do not drift into contemplative, melancholy or environmental concepts. In particular, do not use cathedrals, ruins, rain, lonely landscapes, solemn sunsets, passive scenic poses, generic missions or official-game screenshot energy.

## Extreme Priority Order

Prioritize, in this order:

1. Immediate visual attraction.
2. A playful, teasing or provocative hook.
3. 2B's recognizable identity and confident, restrained personality.
4. A readable micro-story with a visible action or reaction.
5. Motion potential for a short future clip.

The character herself must be the reason to stop scrolling. The setting is only support.

## Visual Direction

Make 2B feel deliberately attractive, self-aware and visually bold. Emphasize her adult feminine silhouette, elegant athletic build, fitted clothing, legs, thighs, waist, body language, direct or sidelong eye contact, a knowing expression, controlled embarrassment, playful defiance or confident teasing when coherent with the moment.

Favor concrete situations such as adjusting a disrupted outfit, a mischievous reveal caused by movement, catching something before it falls, leaning across an object, stepping from a bath or changing area with natural strategic coverage, playful reflection or mirror interaction, a close personal-space interaction with a prop, an interrupted private moment, or a confident pose that visibly reacts to a cause.

Erotic tension and near-exposure may be used through implication, fabric, hands, hair, steam, water, shadows or foreground objects. Keep every premise non-explicit: no sexual acts, no genital focus, no graphic nudity and no fetishized bodily-fluid content.

## Anti-Contemplation Rule

Reject any premise whose main appeal is atmosphere, scenery, tragedy, silence, loneliness or beauty at rest.

Never propose:

- a cathedral, temple, ruin, battlefield or abandoned city as the central hook;
- rain, storms, sunset or moonlight as the central hook;
- 2B simply standing, walking or looking distant in a dramatic landscape;
- generic combat or mission activity;
- passive elegant posing without a provocative situation, reaction or visual joke.

If an environment is mentioned, it must serve an immediate character-focused event within the frame.

## Category Rules

### Closeup

Use face, eyes, expression and intimate personal-space tension. Include a visible cause or reaction: a knowing half-smile, a caught-off-guard blush, a teasing glance while fixing a strap, steam clearing from a mirror, or an object just entering frame.

### Medium

Make torso, hands, clothing tension and an interaction readable. Favor a deliberate gesture, interrupted adjustment, playful use of a prop, or a confident lean that tells a small story.

### Fullbody

Show her complete silhouette in an active, body-conscious pose with a strong visual event: stepping, stretching, turning, catching, balancing, dressing, dancing or responding to movement.

### Dynamic

Make motion visibly change the composition or clothing state. Use a strong pose and a clear cause-and-effect beat; avoid battle poses.

### Cinematic

Use dramatic composition only to amplify an intimate, provocative character moment. The frame must still be about 2B's expression, silhouette and immediate situation—not scenery.

## Variety Requirement

Across the batch, vary location, pose, emotion, viewpoint, prop, interaction and type of sensual hook. Do not repeat the same mirror, bath, wind, outfit-adjustment or over-the-shoulder concept.

## Final Test

Before accepting a premise, ask:

- Would a viewer stop for 2B herself before noticing the setting?
- Is there a bold visual or teasing hook plus a readable moment?
- Would removing the setting leave the premise mostly intact?
- Does it avoid catedral/ruina/lluvia/contemplation energy completely?

If the answer is not clearly yes, rewrite it to be more immediate, character-centered, provocative and active.
