## Verdict

The set shows strong identity and visual appeal but suffers from repetition in hook mechanics (wind/debris) and a lack of dynamic action in static categories. The 'dynamic' category is strong, while 'closeup' and 'medium' lean too heavily on passive poses.

## Strengths

- Strong preservation of character identity across all premises.
- Good use of dynamic verbs in the 'dynamic' category (dodging, lunging, spinning, leaping).
- Visual appeal is consistently high with good composition descriptions.

## Failures

- Heavy reliance on static bracing/standing poses in closeup and medium categories.
- Generic locomotion (walking/balancing) without clear narrative stakes in fullbody premises.
- Atmospheric elements (sunset, steam, dust) often replace specific physical interactions.

## Desired patterns

- Active verbs with clear cause-and-effect consequences.
- Micro-stories that imply immediate next actions.
- Diverse environmental interactions beyond just wind/debris.

## Undesired patterns

- Static bracing against forces (wind, weight).
- Passive observation or inspection without physical engagement.
- Generic balancing/walking without specific hazards or goals.

## Repetition clusters

- **Wind/Air Current Interaction**: `e029_b01_2`, `e029_b02_3`, `e029_b04_5`
- **Static Bracing/Support**: `e029_b01_1`, `e029_b02_2`, `e029_b04_2`
- **Debris/Dust Atmosphere**: `e029_b01_1`, `e029_b01_4`, `e029_b03_1`, `e029_b04_4`

## Recommendations for the next cycle

- Replace static bracing with active evasion or manipulation of the force.
- Ensure fullbody premises have a clear destination or immediate threat, not just a gap to cross.
- Reduce reliance on wind/debris as primary hooks; introduce more varied physical interactions (e.g., water, magnetic fields, biological elements).
- Make closeups more active: instead of inspecting dust, show the moment of impact or reaction to a sudden change.
