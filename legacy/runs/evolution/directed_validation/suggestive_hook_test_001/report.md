# Ada Evolution — Directed Validation Test 001

## Scope

This validation generated 20 non-explicit 2B premises using only the supplied **Directed Candidate v1.1: Suggestive Visual Hook**. It did not run Ada's free-evolution loop, alter any productive guide, call ComfyUI, or generate images.

## Result

**Verdict: positive for directed premise validation.**

| Metric | Directed test | run_027 reference | Reading |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Suggestive Hook | 8.4 | not measured | Strong and explicit project priority |
| Scroll Stop Potential | 8.3 | not measured | Strong; every scene has an immediate character-centered question |
| Sensual Composition | 8.2 | not measured | Present through silhouette, posture, restraint, and framing—not anatomy-first framing |
| Character Identity | 9.4 | 9.0 | Maintained and slightly strengthened by the blindfold, dress, gloves, sleeves, hair, and controlled demeanor |
| Micro Story | 7.9 | 8.0 | Essentially maintained with smaller, more intimate causal chains |
| Animation Potential | 7.5 | 8.5 | Intentionally lower: contained motion replaces run_027's high-kinetic danger/action |
| Diversity | 9.1 | 7.0 | Improved through unique hook/cause/location/gesture/emotion combinations |

## What improved over run_027

- **Immediate attraction and curiosity:** every premise starts with a private, interrupted, playful, or quietly unexplained character moment. The environment is not needed for the hook to survive.
- **Sensuality without generic fanservice:** appeal comes from body language, wardrobe behavior, compositional conceal/reveal, and 2B's composure under a small disruption.
- **Viral-seed readability:** each idea is graspable in one frame and leaves a contained question: an unknown note, object, sound, light, shadow, or interruption.
- **Repetition control:** no climbing, wind-driven action, industrial spectacle, mirror loop, steam loop, or repeated cinematic peril appears as a hook family.

## What remains from run_027

- Identity remains specific and canonical to the local profile; the blindfold is never used to reveal the eyes.
- Every premise contains one visible trigger, a response, and a consequence.
- Movement remains usable for image-to-video: pivots, catch/release, fabric settling, short turns, door motion, and prop movement.
- Micro-stories remain legible, but their stakes are personal and immediate rather than action-driven.

## Trade-off

The test deliberately gives up some of run_027's extreme kinetic animation energy (8.5) to remove trailer syndrome. That is an intended trade: the motion now animates the attractive moment instead of becoming the reason to watch.

## Penalty audit

| Penalty | Result | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Trailer Syndrome | None | No combat, climbing, falling, industrial danger, spectacle, or survival stakes drive a premise. |
| Generic Fanservice | None | No anatomy-first framing, explicit content, or empty “sexy pose” logic. |
| Beauty Shot | None | Each frame has a trigger, response, consequence, and unresolved character question. |
| Repetition | None | The guide's diversity ledger was applied across hook family, cause, location, gesture, emotion, and composition. |

## Conclusion

The directed guide corrects run_027's central failure at the premise level: **the attractive, suggestive character situation is now the main hook, while causal motion and setting support it.**

The recommended next step is to formalize this guide as the seed for a controlled evolution cycle, then validate the same direction with a small image/video sample before any production promotion.
